Case study · GoHighLevel + n8n

AI lead-to-booking system

From form fill to booked call, with no manual follow-up.

The problem

Small service businesses lose leads in the gap between "someone filled in a form" and "someone booked a call". Follow-up is manual, slow and easy to forget, and sales calls rarely get written up.

I built a starter system that closes that gap automatically, using GoHighLevel for the CRM and n8n with AI for call summaries.

How it works

  1. Lead fills in the form A funnel page with a lead form and separate SMS and email consent checkboxes.
  2. Contact is tagged and a deal is created A workflow adds a "new lead" tag and opens an opportunity in the first pipeline stage.
  3. Automatic follow-up A welcome email goes out with the booking link, then a timed reminder if no call is booked.
  4. Call is booked Booking on the calendar moves the deal forward and stops the reminders.
  5. AI answers questions A Conversation AI bot handles common FAQs and hands off to a person when needed.
  6. AI summarizes the sales call An n8n workflow sends the call notes to an AI agent that returns a structured summary and next steps.

What I built

CRM Pipeline, tags & calendar
Automations 3 GHL workflows
AI FAQ bot + call summarizer
Tools GoHighLevel, n8n, Gemini

What I learned

  • Test every workflow with a real test contact and check what actually ran, not just what was set up.
  • Compliance comes first in the US: consent checkboxes, STOP opt-out, quiet hours and email authentication.
  • Structured AI output (fixed fields) makes summaries easy to reuse in a CRM.
  • Document the build so it can be rebuilt quickly for the next client.
