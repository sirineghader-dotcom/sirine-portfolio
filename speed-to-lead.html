Case study · n8n + GoHighLevel + Claude

Speed-to-lead revenue engine

Every lead answered in about a minute, and a scorecard that shows which source is worth the money.

The problem

A B2B agency gets leads from Meta ads, its website and referrals. Replies took hours, half of booked calls no-showed, and nobody knew what a booked call cost.

I built the system that replies in minutes, qualifies each lead with AI, books the call, sends reminders and chases every lead. It also shows the owner which source brings the cheapest booked calls.

How it works

  1. A lead arrives from one of 3 sources Meta lead ads, the website form or a referral form. Each one is tagged with its source.
  2. Claude qualifies it Qualified, Not Qualified or Needs Review, with a 0-100 score and a one-line reason, against a written ideal-client profile.
  3. The reply goes out in about a minute Qualified leads get a booking link by SMS and email. Others get a polite reply, and the team is pinged for anything that needs review.
  4. Booked, rescheduled, cancelled or no-show Each moves the deal to the right stage. Reminders go out 24 hours and 2 hours before the call, and a no-show gets a re-book sequence.
  5. The owner sees which source pays A hosted dashboard shows cost per lead, per booked call and per show, plus the no-show rate and reply speed. It is split by source and never blended.

The hard parts

  • The same event can arrive 3 times. A database lock lets exactly one copy through, and every message is reserved in a send ledger before it goes out, so nobody gets the same message twice.
  • A reschedule kills the old reminders. Reminders are tied to a version of the appointment and re-checked right before sending.
  • A human reply stops everything. Any message the system didn't send pauses all automation for that lead.
  • No silent errors. Crashes, refused messages and quiet problems (like a lead with no reply after 5 minutes) all reach Slack and email.

23 automated checks prove each guarantee against a real database.
